Scope: notable characters in the **Tingen City arc** (Book 1 / early 灰雾 arc, ~1349 in-world). All Beyonder Sequences below are **pinned to the Tingen timeframe**, not their end-of-series state. "Advances later" is noted where a character rises after the arc. Reminder on canon: pathways are named by their Sequence 0; a "Sequence name" is a rank within a pathway, not the pathway itself.

## Loss-of-Control Precedents (the monster-transformation canon)

These are the Tingen-arc canonical models for a Beyonder losing control → becoming a monster → being put down by the Nighthawks. Directly relevant to a Madness/transformation game mechanic.

- **Old Neil / Neil Oram — Hermit pathway, Seq 9 Mystery Pryer.**
  - *Why:* Over-reach + external corruption. He pursued forbidden rituals (an "Alchemical Life") to resurrect his dead fiancée Celeste, and was steadily corrupted by the Hermit-pathway god **"Hidden/Hermit Sage" (隐匿贤者)**, who whispers unassimilable knowledge into Hermit Beyonders (especially during advancement) — failure to assimilate turns them into a lunatic or an outright monster.
  - *Resolution:* Klein confronted him and exposed the obsession as delusion; Neil still mutated into a monster and was **killed by captain Dunn Smith**. Precedent for: *pollution/whisper-driven madness + mercy-kill by the team.*

- **Rear / Rial Bieber — Antigonus Family Notebook (raw ancestral ingestion).**
  - *Why:* **Raw ingestion of un-digested power.** Drawn by the notebook (a dangerous Sealed Artifact holding ancestral Beyonder legacy), he tried to "digest" his ancestors' bequest without proper adaptation, half-mad, hiding in Tirrel Warehouse.
  - *Resolution:* Nighthawks found his ritual site and **interrupted the digestion → immediate loss of control** into a >2m humanoid monster (joints reversed, bones jutting, a "peeled brain" surface). A Nighthawk (Rozanne/洛络塔) ripped the notebook out of his gut; cut off from its power source, he **self-detonated and died**. The team recovered his supernatural material (a fist-sized brain-like grey mass — likely a Beyonder Characteristic). Precedent for: *interrupted/incomplete digestion of raw power → instant monster; kill by severing the power source.*

- **General canon on WHY Beyonders lose control** (from the same sources, useful for mechanic design): drinking the next-sequence potion before the current one is **digested**; drinking potions from **non-adjacent / mismatched pathways**; **poor mental state**; **external corruption/pollution** (e.g., a pathway god's whispers); and **over-use** of powers. The underlying nature of Beyonder power is "chaos and madness" — loss of control = total mental+physical collapse into a high-lethality monster.

- **Daly Simone (finale)** is a softer variant: she loses control from a **forced spirit-world pact** during the hunt for Ince Zangwill and is suppressed/killed via puppetization — i.e., *over-reach under duress* rather than raw ingestion.

## Confidence Caveats (top 3)

1. **Daly Simone's exact Tingen-present sequence (Seq 6 vs Seq 7).** Her potion-history is well documented (Seq8→Seq7→Seq6 over several years), but sources emphasize her *public* "Spiritualist/Spirit Medium" (Seq7) identity. As a veteran she is most plausibly **Seq 6 Necromancer** by the arc's present day; flagged **medium**. Cross-check the novel text for the precise year if the game needs it exact.
2. **Klein's mid-arc timing (Seer → Clown).** He is definitively Seq9 Seer on joining and Seq8 Clown after the Magician kill; the exact chapter of advancement within Tingen wasn't re-verified line-by-line. His "The Fool" persona must not be mistaken for a real Seq0 rank in this arc.
3. **Ince Zangwill's pathway state is transitional.** He is Death-pathway **Seq5 Doorkeeper** during the arc but switches to Darkness and advances to **Seq4 Night Watcher** through the finale itself — so his "Tingen sequence" is a moving target depending on whether you pin pre- or post-finale.
