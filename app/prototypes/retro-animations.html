TOMB OF NASSAK AN-RAH

Animation prototypes — Rod Lord litho style (diagram + label + reveal) and outline-wireframe 3D under the terminal's CRT dressing. All self-contained: no libraries, no assets, quantized to hand-made frame steps.

================================================================

01 — THE MANIFOLD BOX

WIREFRAME / HYPERGEOMETRY

A tesseract projected 4D→3D→2D, rotating through the planes the tomb does not otherwise have. Inner cell dimmed by w-depth. This cannot be modeled from an illustration, because that is the joke. Attaches: EXAMINE MANIFOLD BOX, the ulfire reveal.

02 — THE PRISMATIC BLADE

WIREFRAME / OBJECT CARD

A guard's prismatic blade: faceted tip, square section, plain soldier's hilt. One facet edge cycles through the spectrum in steps — the edge bends light, and will snap at the hilt exactly once. Attaches: EXAMINE PRISMATIC BLADE; the coffin pry.

03-C — THE CANOPIC SYSTEM, SUPINE

LITHO / VARIANT

The procedure as the table sees it: the patient lying flat above the jar row, trimmed at the shoulders, organs descending one by one. No buzzer. Alternate for 03.

04 — GUIDE CARD: PTHALO-JACKAL

LITHO / CREATURE ENTRY

A field-guide entry that annotates itself: cycling callouts, a PATIENCE gauge stepping down, tribute advice arriving just in time. Attaches: first jackal encounter; the hint booklet's jackal topic.

05 — THE WHEEL OF BATS

PARTICLES / ENDING

The colony wheels over the tomb, ten turns, then scatters toward the horizon's molten line — and resets. Forty strokes, one ellipse, per-bat jitter. Attaches: the dates-under-open-sky beat; a death card.

05-B — BATS: THE DARK ROOM

LITHO / JUMP-SCARE

The hall as the game meets it: black, with only the probe-senses arriving as text — then a glowstone blooms for two seconds, the statues and the seething ceiling exist, and the card cuts back to black: MISTAKE. Alternate for 05 (the interior).

05-C — BATS: ONE BAT, PORTRAIT

LITHO / CLOSE-UP

One of the eleven hundred, hanging folded: wing membranes with the finger bones showing through, breathing slow — and as the LIGHT gauge fills, one eye opens. Alternate for 05 (the individual).

05-D — BATS: EVACUATION DIAGRAM

LITHO / SAFETY CARD

A safety-card parody: IN THE EVENT OF LIGHT, the colony's dive paths in numbered arrows, the marked safe route along the wall — and step three typing itself out: you are the exit. Alternate for 05 (the briefing).

06 — THE BURIAL CYLINDERS

LITHO / HALL OF WARRIORS

Four plexiglas cylinders, guard-mummies at an attention no order will relieve. Gel in three gel-filter hues, bubbles on the step clock, fungus veining the orange one like pressed flowers, and the burst one already spilled. Callouts cycle the kit. Attaches: entering the Hall of Warriors; EXAMINE CYLINDERS.

06-B — THE BURIAL CYLINDERS, INTACT

LITHO / VARIANT

The hall as the tombwrights left it: all four sealed, four gel-filter hues, four guards at attention, the fungus not yet arrived at the orange glass. Before anyone came scavenging. Alternate for 06 (the before).

08 — THE GLOWSTONE

WIREFRAME / INTERACTIVE

A shard of cold lazulite with a switch. CLICK THE SCREEN to throw it: the stone steps up to a warm yellow burn, and the ATTENTION gauge fills while it is lit — light is dear, and attention dearer. It also demonstrates itself. Attaches: LIGHT / DOUSE GLOWSTONE; the tutorial's first lesson.

09 — SILAS

LITHO / CHARACTER CARD

The synthetic archivist at his reading: yellow-stippled robes (so the dust shows), a paneled synth skull in the hood, the fungus port beneath the jaw — and the lattice's bright threads visibly spooling under his fingertips while the remembered days accrue. Attaches: TALK TO SILAS; the Hall of Memory.

10 — SYNTH-HOUND

LITHO / PRESERVED SPECIMEN

Dead, and kept that way: a coursing unit suspended in the luminous embalming gel behind the plexiglas, goggled eye dark, piston legs limp, drifting by millimetres on the tank's own slow currents. Bubbles on the step clock. Attaches: the Hall of Hounds tank; EXAMINE HOUNDS.

11-B — THE BURIAL SPHERE, UPRIGHT

LITHO / VARIANT

The same chamber, but the coffin floats UPRIGHT — a standing glass egg with a vertical hairline seam, the tenant coiling tall inside it, the fungus strands meeting its crown. Alternate for 11.

12 — FRIEND'S FUNGUS

LITHO / PHARMACOPOEIA

A pink cluster, soft and faintly warm: agreeability radiating off the caps in quantized rings, spores drifting up, and — briefly, if you watch — small smiles among the motes. The Autarchy fed it to guests of state. Attaches: SEARCH CORPSE at the Summit; TASTE FUNGUS (the microdose).

13 — THE AUTARCH

LITHO / BEATIFIC SLUMBER

The peace state: eyes closed at last, the carved lightning still, the beard still — nothing moves except one slow breathing pulse through the whole stone, the pace of a man dreaming something kind. Attaches: the Prayer of Peaceful Slumber; the FIX COFFIN ending.

13-C — THE AUTARCH, HOLLOWED

LITHO / VARIANT

The furthest gone: the stone worn to the skull beneath — nasal aperture, a grin of carved teeth (one missing) — and the sockets FULL of the orange, pulsing, with sprigs of it testing the air. The ropes still work the bust. Alternate for 13 (the ending nobody wanted).

14 — THE MANTIS JAR

LITHO / CLOSE-UP

Split, budding, and listening: the crack creeps wider on the step clock, the antennae twitch at nothing, and when the SONG gauge fills the jar sings — rasp-rings radiating — and something in the halls goes very still. Attaches: EXAMINE MANTIS JAR; the song trigger.

15 — THE JACKAL JAR

LITHO / CLOSE-UP

The brain, strangely: a cutaway window on the jarred thoughts, pulsing at a reading pace, while the stopper's tall ears twitch — still listening, four thousand years on. Attaches: EXAMINE JACKAL JAR; TASTE BRAIN.

16 — THE FALCON JAR

LITHO / CLOSE-UP

The hooked ceremonial stopper with its slow nictitating blink, and through the cutaway, the coil of cured intestines turning by degrees. Last worn as a helm by the spawn of guts. Attaches: EXAMINE FALCON JAR; the tribute economy.

17 — EXTERIOR, TAKE ONE: THE APPROACH

LITHO / SCENE

The Moebius composition: a vast dusk, the monolith alone on the horizon's molten line, tendrils curling off its crown — and a scavenger the size of a word, walking toward the door, then stopping to look up. Attaches: the title screen; arriving at the Tomb Exterior.

17-B — THE APPROACH, THE OLD MAN

LITHO / VARIANT

The same dusk and the same small walker — but the crown of the monolith is the old man in profile, turned to the sky, mouth open, the orange weeping out of it in slow curls. His sentence continues underground. Alternate for 17.

17-C — THE APPROACH, THIN CURLS

LITHO / VARIANT

17-B's monolith — the old man's profile, mouth open to the sky — but wearing 17's original tendrils: thin wandering curls instead of ropes, rising out of the mouth like handwriting. Alternate for 17.

23 — SPAWN OF BRAIN: THE LISTENER

LITHO / CREATURE

The reference, alive: the jackal jar worn as a head over a risen brain of a body on wading-bird legs — and when a sound ripples at the edge of the frame, the whole creature LEANS toward it, ears pricking. Attaches: the Spawn's sound-hunting; EXAMINE SPAWN OF BRAIN.

24 — SPAWN OF BRAIN: MID-STEP

LITHO / WALKING

The stalk: high bird-steps on the gait clock, the brain-body jouncing with every stride — while the jar head holds PERFECTLY level, the way a heron's does. Attaches: the Spawn homing on a noise.

25 — SPAWN OF BRAIN: FELLED

LITHO / AS FOUND

As the game finds it: felled mid-step, legs where the stride left them, the jar rolled to its side and rocking itself still, the last thoughts pulsing out of the body and fading. Attaches: the Hall of Youth's opening scene.

26 — SPAWN OF GUTS: THE SWAY

LITHO / CREATURE

The falcon jar worn as a helm over a face of decorative eyes, a collar of gut, a corrugated hose of an arm braced on the floor, and a skirt of tendrils that never stops moving. It sways toward whatever you just did. Attaches: EXAMINE SPAWN OF GUTS; the sound-hunt.

27 — SPAWN OF GUTS: THE DRIP

LITHO / CLOSE-UP

The face, closer than anyone wants: seven eyes blinking out of sequence (they are decorative; it hunts by ear), the gut-collar pulsing, and the drip on the step clock, each drop finding the floor. Attaches: the mantis-song fight; a damage card.

28 — SPAWN OF GUTS: THE CLAIM

LITHO / THE LOOT

The story beat: the spawn goes down into its own skirt of tendrils, and the falcon jar topples forward, bounces once, and settles upright — waiting to be claimed for the plinth. Attaches: felling the spawn; the falcon jar award.
